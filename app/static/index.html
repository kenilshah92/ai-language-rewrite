What stays the same
  • PDF: page dimensions, images, and graphics. Wide paragraphs reflow within their original area. Unsupported fonts, overlaps, and text that does not fit are skipped. Scanned PDFs need a separate OCR workflow.
  • Word (.docx): font and style settings, tables, links, and image files. Complex fields, review markup, text boxes, and fixed-height rows stay original. Word may repaginate after edits; review the result in Word.
  • Plain text (.txt): UTF-8 encoding, blank lines, line endings, and indentation. There are no fonts or images in this format.

The editing rules include guidance from Wikipedia’s Signs of AI writing. These are writing-quality guidelines, not an AI-detector guarantee. Legacy .doc files and other formats are not supported yet.
